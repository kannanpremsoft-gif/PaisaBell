PaisaBell Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

PaisaBell (“the app”) is operated by Premsoft Software Technologies. PaisaBell: Every Payment, Every Ping.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email and account ID when you sign in with Google (Firebase Authentication).
  • Phone number: your mobile number, verified by a one-time password (OTP).
  • Subscription records: plan, order ID, payment status and dates. Payments are made through Razorpay, and we do not receive or store your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Device token: used for push messages (Firebase Cloud Messaging).

We do not collect your UPI PIN, bank passwords, card numbers, CVV, bank OTPs, contacts, location or advertising ID.

2. Notification access

PaisaBell uses Android Notification Access, only after you grant it. The app reads notifications from supported payment apps and messages to detect an incoming payment and speak the amount aloud with your phone’s text-to-speech. This content is used on your device only to create the announcement and show your recent payments list. It is not used for advertising or shared with others. You can turn this permission off at any time in Android settings.

A foreground service with a visible notification keeps voice alerts working when the screen is off.

3. QR sticker feature

Photos you pick or take of your QR code, and the stickers you design, are processed and saved on your device only. The camera is opened through your phone’s own camera app. PaisaBell does not access your camera directly.

4. How we use information

To create and secure your account, verify your phone number, provide voice alerts and payment history, manage your subscription, send service messages and meet legal requirements. We do not sell your personal data.

5. Third-party services

  • Google / Firebase (sign-in and push messages)
  • Supabase (account and subscription records)
  • Razorpay (subscription payments)
  • OTP / SMS provider (phone verification)

These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep account and subscription records while your account is active and as required by law. To request deletion of your account and data, email us at the address below from your registered email. We will respond within 30 days, except where records must be kept by law.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections. No system is completely secure. Never share your UPI PIN, bank password, CVV or bank OTP with anyone.

8. Children

PaisaBell is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your data, and withdraw your consent at any time, by contacting us. Residents of India have the rights provided under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page with a new effective date.
